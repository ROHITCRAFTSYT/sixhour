The problem

When a small business is hacked, two legal clocks start. Nobody in the office can read the logs.

6 h

CERT-In incident report

CERT-In Directions, 28 Apr 2022 (IT Act s.70B). Applies to every body corporate. Report within 6 hours of noticing; keep 180 days of logs in India.

72 h

DPDP breach report

DPDP Rules 2025, Rule 7 (in force 13 May 2027). Tell the Data Protection Board and every affected person without delay; detailed report in 72 hours. Penalty ceiling up to ₹200 crore.

A 40-person exporter, diagnostic lab or loan agency has one IT person, no SOC, no lawyer on call. Today they either miss the deadline, file nothing, or call a consultant after the clock has already run out.

The product

Paste the logs. Get the incident, the clock, and the filings.

  1. Privacy first. Aadhaar, PAN, phone, email and card numbers are masked before any line reaches a model.
  2. Reconstructs the attack with Gemini: what, when, from where, which systems, was personal data touched. Every finding links to the log line that proves it.
  3. Starts both clocks and shows time left.
  4. Drafts the filings: CERT-In report, Data Protection Board intimation, and a plain-language notice to affected people in Tamil, Hindi and 6 more languages.
  5. Tells the IT person what to do in the next hour.

Privacy notice.

SixHour is built by LorqAI. It is designed to handle as little personal data as a breach-response tool can.

What we handle

DataWhyKept
Logs you paste or dropTo analyse the incidentNot kept. Held in memory for the length of the request.
Organisation and contact details you typeTo fill in the draftsNot kept on our servers. Saved in your own browser until you close the incident.
Your IP addressRate limiting and abuse preventionHeld in memory for about a minute. Our hosting provider also keeps standard access logs.

Personal data inside your logs

Your logs may contain personal data of your own customers or staff. For that data you are the Data Fiduciary and SixHour acts on your instructions. Before analysis, Aadhaar numbers, PAN, phone numbers, email addresses and card numbers are replaced with tokens. The originals are not sent to any AI model and are not stored.

Who else processes it

  • Vercel hosts the public desk.
  • Google (Gemini API) processes masked log lines, the organisation name and sector, and any notice you ask to translate, when AI is switched on. You can switch it off on the desk.

We do not sell data, show advertising, or use analytics or tracking cookies.

Your choices

  • Use the rules engine alone by unticking "Use Gemini".
  • Delete the incident saved in your browser with "Close incident".
  • Run SixHour on your own machine from the open source.

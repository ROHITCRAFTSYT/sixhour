1. A drafting aid, not legal advice

SixHour produces drafts and an analysis to help you respond to a security incident. It is not a law firm and nothing it produces is legal advice. The drafts follow the structure of the CERT-In incident reporting form and DPDP Rule 7, and they may be incomplete or wrong.

2. You review and you file

You are responsible for checking every draft, for deciding what to report, and for filing on time with CERT-In, the Data Protection Board and anyone else. SixHour never files or sends anything on your behalf. The clocks are calculated from the time you enter; check them against your own records.

3. Only logs you are allowed to share

Paste only logs from systems you own or are authorised to investigate. Do not use the desk to process data you have no right to handle, to attack any system, or to overload the service.
